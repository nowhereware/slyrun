how to play

Quicker you Type
Faster Sly Runs

outrun ember · storm
Step 1

Take the 30-second test

Type a few lines so Slyrun learns your real speed. Everything in the game is tuned to it.

Step 2

Type to run

Every correct letter pushes Sly forward. Type faster and Sly sprints. Slow down and Sly tires.

Step 3

Stay ahead of the Storm

It never stops. If it reaches Sly, the ground gives way.

Typos cost you ground. Each typo takes one of Sly's three lives, then you get 3 seconds before the next typo counts. Finish a word to win a life back. Lose all three and the Storm catches Sly.

modes

Pick your run

30 s · 1 min · 2 min

Timed

Pick a length and survive until the clock counts down to zero without the Storm catching Sly. Your score is your words per minute and accuracy.

Until you're caught

Journey

No time limit. Keep typing for as long as you can; the clock counts up until the Storm catches Sly. Your score is your words per minute and accuracy.

fair panic

A 30 WPM typist and a 120 WPM typist feel the same panic.

Most typing games crush slow typists or bore fast ones. Slyrun sets the Storm's pace from your own speed, so everyone runs on the edge.

  • Typed slowly on the test to get an easy run? The Storm learns your real pace within seconds, then lunges.
  • Ranked runs use fixed speeds, so every leaderboard score is earned.

And you get faster without noticing.

How Slyrun keeps up with you

Type faster→Sly pulls ahead

Smooth, not twitchy

Sly's speed follows your typing over the last 3 seconds, so Sly never stutters between words.

It learns while you run

Type well above your baseline and your baseline rises mid-run. The Storm speeds up with it.
